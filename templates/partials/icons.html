{#- The browser-tab and home-screen icons: the logo's houses mark. Dark marks on a light system, light marks on a dark one — decided by the OS scheme, not the ffm.theme cookie, because the tab bar the icon sits on is the browser's chrome and follows the OS, not the page. The SVG switches itself with a prefers-color-scheme rule inside it; the PNGs are for a browser without SVG favicons and switch with `media` on the link. The touch icon is an opaque tile, because iOS fills transparency with black. -#} {#- Installing it on a phone — see `web::pwa` for the manifest. iOS reads none of the manifest's display or name: `apple-mobile-web-app-capable` is what launches it full screen from the home screen, and each page sets its own `apple-mobile-web-app-title`, or the icon would be named after the tab title, month and all. `default` keeps the system's own status bar above the page rather than drawing the page under it, where its white text would vanish on a light palette. -#}
