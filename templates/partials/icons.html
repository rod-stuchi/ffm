{#- The browser-tab and home-screen icons: the logo's houses mark. Dark marks on a light system, light marks on a dark one — decided by the OS scheme, not the ffm.theme cookie, because the tab bar the icon sits on is the browser's chrome and follows the OS, not the page. The SVG switches itself with a prefers-color-scheme rule inside it; the PNGs are for a browser without SVG favicons and switch with `media` on the link. The touch icon is an opaque tile, because iOS fills transparency with black. -#}
