{#- `:grep` — the result list, in the same column every other pane uses. Searching every month is a different verb from `/`: one can answer without a round trip and the other cannot, so overloading them would make the fast one slow. It opens with the keyboard on the first hit: `j`/`k`/`gg`/`G` walk the list, Enter (or a click) goes to the hit — its month, its member's pane, the row itself — and closes the split. Each hit is still a real link carrying all three, so a Cmd-click opens it in a tab of its own. The methods are `gp`-prefixed because this scope chains onto the shell's, and Alpine merges data and methods into one namespace. -#}
