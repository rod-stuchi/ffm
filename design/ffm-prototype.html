FFM

Family Finances Manager

Interactive design prototype · example household data

A working model of the ledger, not a picture of one. Click into the app below and drive it from the keyboard: jk to move, 5j to count, HL to switch member panels, x to select, dd to soft-delete, u to undo, / for the command bar. Everything is also clickable. Amounts, dates and receipt filenames follow Brazilian conventions.

01

The ledger

FFM_001

One continuous transactions table, sliced by month — click the month to jump anywhere in 2026. Each household member gets a panel with its own INs/OUTs header; the focused panel carries the accent ring, and the focused row carries the gutter zero. The rnu at the far right of the statusline is the gutter mode: relative numbers, as in Vim's :set relativenumber. Click it to cycle.

04

Keymap

NORMAL MODE

Lowercase acts on the occurrence, uppercase on the broader rule or window — the same split Vim uses. Nothing destructive fires from a single keystroke.

Names, amounts and filenames are example data for the prototype. Two keys are proposals rather than decisions from the spec: p toggles a transaction private (hidden from other members, never from admin), and gg / G jump to the first and last row.
